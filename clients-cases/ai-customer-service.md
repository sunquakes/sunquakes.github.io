# AI Customer Service System

Most support questions repeat — but the answers live scattered across documents, wikis and the heads of a few experienced staff. This system puts an AI assistant in front of the support channel: it answers from the company's own knowledge base, shows where each answer came from, and hands the conversation to a human when it can't help.

## The challenge

- **The same questions, every day.** Staff spend hours answering questions that were already answered yesterday — and every answer depends on someone being available.
- **Answers live everywhere and nowhere.** Product docs, policies and troubleshooting notes are spread across files and folders, so even internal staff search before they can reply.
- **No coverage outside working hours.** Customers ask in the evening or on weekends and wait until the next business day for a reply.
- **Inconsistent answers.** Two staff members can give two different answers to the same question, and customers notice.

## Result

- Repetitive questions are answered instantly and consistently, any hour of the day — staff time goes to the questions that actually need a person.
- Answers come with their sources, so customers trust the reply and staff stop re-checking it.
- Unanswered questions became a to-do list for the knowledge base — the documentation improves where customers actually get stuck.
