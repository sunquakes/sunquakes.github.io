# Services & Pricing

## Full Service List

### Custom Business Software Solutions

- Bespoke web applications and branded business websites
- Scalable backend APIs to connect your existing business systems
- Automated workflows and data processing to cut manual repetitive work
- Third-party system integrations and webhook connections
- Website migration, speed optimisation and reliability improvements
- Code audit, architecture review and technical advisory
- Ongoing support and maintenance retainer packages

### AI Business Automation

- Custom AI agents to handle multi-step operational tasks
- Knowledge-base chatbots for internal teams or customer self-service
- AI-powered data extraction, classification and document processing
- Connect AI capabilities into your existing tools and business workflows
- Build guardrails and safe operating boundaries for AI business tasks

### 3D Interactive Business Experiences

**Web & Client 3D Solutions**

- Interactive 3D product viewers and online product configurators
- Optimised 3D assets, scene setup and 3D animation sequences
- 3D data preparation, asset cleanup and format conversion pipelines
- 3D rendering workflows, pre-rendered visual assets and scene exports
- Performance tuning for web browsers and client-side delivery
- Custom interactive 3D experiences accessible via web or client interfaces

### GIS & Map Solutions

- Interactive web maps and map-based business dashboards
- 3D GIS scenes — terrain, buildings and city models rendered in the browser
- GIS data processing, cleanup and format conversion (Shapefile, GeoJSON, KML, GeoTIFF)
- Spatial queries, geocoding and route or distance analysis
- Tile and vector map layers with custom styling
- Map features integrated into your existing web apps and systems
- Performance tuning for large spatial and 3D datasets

### Additional Implementation Services

- Cloud deployment, domain, DNS and SSL setup
- Basic search optimisation and page performance tuning
- User guides and operational documentation
- Training sessions for your team to manage and operate new tools
